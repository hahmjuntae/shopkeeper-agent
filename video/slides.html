Shopkeeper

A billing assistant for one-person businesses.
Type one sentence. Shopkeeper does the PayPal work.

Small jobs, many forms

  • A tutor or a freelance designer gets paid for many small jobs.
  • Each job needs an invoice, a due date, and often a reminder.
  • In the dashboard, one invoice takes many screens and form fields.
  • Owners skip the reminder. The money comes late.
